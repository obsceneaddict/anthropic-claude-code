Adults only

Rose Noir sells products intended for adults. You must be 18 or older to browse the catalogue or place an order. This is not a formality we resent — it is the whole basis on which we are allowed to operate.

How we handle age

When you first arrive, we ask you to confirm you are 18 or older. That confirmation is stored in your own browser for the current session only; it is never transmitted to us and never linked to your identity. When you place an order, you confirm it again as part of accepting our terms of sale.

We deliberately do not ask for a date of birth or an identity document to browse. Collecting identity data we do not need would make your privacy worse, not better.

If we believe a buyer is underage

We will cancel the order and refund it in full. We may decline future orders from the same address or payment method. We would rather lose a sale than complete that one.

Deliveries

Parcels are plain and unbranded, with nothing on the label indicating the contents. Where a courier offers age-verified handover, we will use it for orders that warrant it. DO WERYFIKACJI: confirm which of your chosen couriers actually offer this in Poland and at what cost.

If you are a parent or guardian

If you share a device with a minor, browser-level and operating-system parental controls are more effective than any check a shop can perform. On iOS see Screen Time; on Android see Family Link; most home routers also support content filtering. We will honour a request to block a specific email address or delivery address from ordering — write to hello@rosenoir.pl.

Reporting a concern

If you believe someone underage has ordered from us, tell us at hello@rosenoir.pl. We investigate every report and we do not need proof to look into it.
