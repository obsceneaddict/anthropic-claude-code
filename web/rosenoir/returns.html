Returns & right of withdrawal

Last updated: 26 July 2026

The short version

You have 14 days to change your mind about most orders. But for hygiene reasons, intimate products that arrived sealed cannot be returned once that seal is broken. This is normal across the industry and it exists to protect the next customer.

1. Right of withdrawal (14 days)

As a consumer you may withdraw from a distance-sale contract within 14 days of receiving the goods, without giving a reason. To do so, tell us in writing — an email to returns@rosenoir.pl is enough. You do not need to use a special form, though we will send you one if you prefer.

Return the goods within 14 days of telling us. You cover the cost of the return shipment unless the item was faulty or we sent the wrong thing.

2. When withdrawal does not apply

Under Polish consumer law (implementing the EU Consumer Rights Directive), the right of withdrawal is excluded for sealed goods which are not suitable for return due to health protection or hygiene reasons, where the seal has been broken after delivery. In practice, for this shop that means:

  • Cannot be returned once opened: toys, anything intended for internal use, lubricants, oils, and the pelvic floor set.
  • Can be returned if unworn and unwashed, with tags and hygiene liner intact: lingerie, robes, silk ties.
  • Can be returned in original condition: restraints, collars, candles, cleaners, and unopened gift sets.

Every affected item ships with a visible seal. If the seal is intact and the item is otherwise unused, we will accept the return.

3. Faulty or damaged goods

The hygiene exclusion does not limit your rights if an item is defective. If something arrives damaged, stops working, or does not match its description, you are covered by statutory warranty rights regardless of whether the seal was broken. Email us with your order number and a photograph and we will arrange a repair, replacement, or refund.

4. Refunds

We refund to your original payment method within 14 days of receiving the returned goods, or of receiving proof that you sent them, whichever is earlier. We refund the product price plus the cheapest standard delivery option we offered — not express delivery, if you chose it.

Refunds appear on your statement under the same neutral trading name as the original charge.

5. How to send something back

  1. Email returns@rosenoir.pl with your order number.
  2. We reply with the return address and, where applicable, a prepaid label.
  3. Pack the item in any plain outer box. Nothing on the parcel identifies the contents, in either direction.

6. Complaints and disputes

If we cannot resolve a complaint between us, you may use out-of-court dispute resolution. Polish consumers can approach the regional trade inspectorate (Wojewódzki Inspektorat Inspekcji Handlowej) or the district consumer ombudsman.
