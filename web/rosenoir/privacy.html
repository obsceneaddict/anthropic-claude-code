Privacy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

The short version

What you buy here is nobody's business but yours. We collect the minimum needed to take an order and get it to you, we never sell it, and we never put anything identifying on the parcel or your bank statement.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is [registered company name, address, NIP, REGON — to be inserted], trading as Rose Noir. Contact: privacy@rosenoir.pl.

2. What we collect and why

Order data — name, delivery address, email, phone
To fulfil your order. Legal basis: performance of a contract. Kept for as long as tax law requires us to keep invoices (currently five years from the end of the tax year).
Payment data
Handled entirely by our payment provider. We receive a confirmation and a transaction reference — never your full card number. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
Email address, if you subscribe
To send occasional restock and new-arrival emails. Legal basis: your consent, withdrawable at any time via the unsubscribe link in every message.
Age confirmation
Your confirmation that you are 18+ is stored in your browser for the session only. It is not transmitted to us.
Basic technical logs
Our host records IP addresses and request times for security and abuse prevention. Legal basis: legitimate interest. Kept short-term.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. Ever.
  • We do not build advertising profiles or share data with ad networks.
  • We do not put product names, categories, or the shop name on parcels or statements.
  • We do not ask for data we do not need — there is no date-of-birth field, no gender field, no "tell us about your preferences".

4. Who else sees your data

Only processors we need to run the shop, each bound by a data-processing agreement:

  • The courier or parcel-locker operator — name, address, phone. They see a delivery label with no indication of contents.
  • The payment provider — handles the transaction; we never hold card details.
  • Our hosting and email providers — store the site and send transactional email.
  • Our accountant — sees invoices, because tax law requires it.

DO WERYFIKACJI: name each provider explicitly once chosen, and confirm whether any transfers data outside the EEA.

5. Cookies and local storage

This site currently uses no advertising or analytics cookies. It stores two things in your own browser, which never leave your device on their own:

  • your 18+ confirmation, for the current session only;
  • your cart contents, so they survive a page reload.

Clearing your browser data removes both. If analytics are added later, this page will be updated first and consent will be requested where required.

6. Your rights

Under GDPR you may ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct anything inaccurate;
  • delete your data, where we are not legally required to keep it (invoices, for instance, we must keep);
  • restrict or object to a particular use;
  • transfer your data to another provider;
  • withdraw marketing consent, at any time, without giving a reason.

Write to privacy@rosenoir.pl and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handled it, you may complain to the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

7. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to order data is limited to people who need it to pack and ship. We keep no card details at any point.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and, for anything significant, tell subscribers by email rather than quietly editing the page.
