How does scoring work?

Pick a moment from the quick library, or type your own — Auranomics scores it instantly, on your device. Bigger, more emotional moments swing the score harder.

How do streaks work?

Log at least one moment on a given day to keep your streak alive. Streaks are based on your device's local calendar day.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open Auranomics → Settings → Upgrade to Pro → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iOS Settings app → your name at the top → Subscriptions → Auranomics → Cancel Subscription. We don't have the ability to cancel it for you — Apple manages all billing.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all my data, inside the app. This is permanent and immediate.
