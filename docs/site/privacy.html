AURANOMICS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Short version: Auranomics does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. Everything you log stays on your device.

What data we collect

Auranomics has no user accounts and no login. The aura events you log (titles, points, categories, timestamps) are stored exclusively in a local file inside the app's private storage on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to any server we operate — the app has no backend.

In-App Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see or store your payment details. Apple's handling of that data is governed by Apple's own privacy policy, available at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Analytics and third parties

The current version of Auranomics does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated first and the change will be reflected in the app's "What's New" notes.

Data deletion

You can permanently delete all logged data at any time from Settings → "Delete all my data." Deleting the app also deletes all locally stored data, since nothing exists outside your device.

Children's privacy

Auranomics is not directed at children under 13. Since the app collects no personal data of any kind, no personal information is knowingly collected from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last updated" date.
